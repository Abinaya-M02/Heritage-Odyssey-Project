About Heritage Odyssey

Heritage Odyssey is an Indian Heritage Information System designed to help users discover and explore India's rich cultural and historical heritage. The platform provides information about UNESCO World Heritage Sites, temples, churches, forts, palaces, museums, monuments, and natural heritage across the country.

It also helps travelers plan their journeys by offering heritage information in one place, making it easier to explore famous destinations, learn about their history, and choose places to visit. Heritage Odyssey aims to promote India's cultural legacy while providing a simple and user-friendly travel experience.
